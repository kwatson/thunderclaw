Connection

Checking connection…

Enter the ThunderClaw endpoint shown by your OpenClaw installation. HTTPS is required except for explicit 127.0.0.1 or [::1] loopback endpoints.

Email data sent when you use ThunderClaw

Compose actions send selected text, the entire visible authored draft body, extracted quoted message history, subject, and recipients. Message actions send the entire visible rendered message body, including visible quoted history and signatures, plus its subject and author. This data passes through your configured OpenClaw agent and its configured model provider. Hooks installed in that OpenClaw Gateway can technically access it.

Pairing stays disabled until you consent. Disconnect or Forget connection to withdraw consent and stop further use.

Your agents

Test the connection to load your agents.

Verifying an agent makes up to two synthetic model calls through its configured provider and may incur provider charges. Verification does not send or use email content.

Advanced settings Permissions, credentials, and recovery

Host permission

Pairing asks Thunderbird to grant access only to the configured scheme and hostname. Thunderbird’s grant covers every port and path on that hostname, while ThunderClaw confines its requests to this endpoint and its fixed routes.

Connection credential

If you suspect this device’s credential has been exposed, replace it without pairing again.

Disconnect or reset

Disconnect confirms remote revocation before removing local access. Use Forget connection only for recovery when OpenClaw cannot be reached.
